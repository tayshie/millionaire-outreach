Forbes Billionaires

Real-time API — names, net worth, industry, country, age, company. ~3,000 billionaires.

Wikipedia Categories

Billionaires, CEOs, tech entrepreneurs, philanthropists, hedge fund managers.

SEC EDGAR Insiders

Insider trading filings (Forms 3/4/5). Includes names, addresses, phone numbers, companies, tickers.

Company Team Pages

Scrapes company websites' /team, /about, /leadership pages. Goldmine for emails & names.

Google News Wealth

Extracts names from financial news. Billionaire/millionaire mentions in current articles.

Google Dork Search

Advanced search queries for "CEO email", "net worth", "billionaire profile" to find contact info.

Bloomberg Billionaires

Bloomberg's billionaire index. Names and rankings scraped from public page.

Hurun Global Rich List

Hurun Report's global rich list. Different coverage from Forbes, especially Asia-heavy.

AngelList Investors

Angel investors and venture capitalists. Wealthy individuals in the startup ecosystem.

OpenCorporates Directors

Corporate registry directors. Company officers (CEOs, presidents, directors) with jurisdictions.
